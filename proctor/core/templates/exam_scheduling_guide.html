📚 Exam Scheduling Guide

Learn how to schedule exams using Google Sheets

📥 Download Question Template

Start by downloading our pre-formatted template

Download QuestionsProctor.xlsx
1 Upload Template to Google Drive

After downloading the template:

  • Go to Google Drive
  • Click "New" → "File upload"
  • Select the downloaded QuestionsProctor.xlsx file
  • Once uploaded, right-click the file and select "Open with Google Sheets"
2 Fill in Your Questions

The template has the following structure:

Template Format

question option1 option2 option3 option4 answer
What is 2+2? 3 4 5 6 4
Capital of France? London Paris Berlin Madrid Paris

Important Rules

• Do NOT change column headers
• The answer column must match exactly with one of the options
• All fields are required - no empty cells
• Use simple text only - no formulas or special characters

3 Share Sheet with Proctor System

This is the MOST IMPORTANT step!

  1. Click the "Share" button in the top-right corner of Google Sheets
  2. In the "Add people and groups" field, paste this email:
proctor-api@proctorsystemapi.iam.gserviceaccount.com
  1. Set the role to "Editor"
  2. Click "Send"

Why This is Needed

The Proctor System needs Editor access to read your questions from Google Sheets. This is a secure service account that only accesses sheets you explicitly share.

4 Copy the Google Sheet Link

After sharing the sheet:

  1. Click the "Share" button again
  2. Click "Copy link"
  3. OR simply copy the URL from your browser's address bar

The link will look like this:

https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1abc...xyz/edit
5 Schedule Your Exam

Go back to the Schedule Exam page and:

  1. Fill in all exam details (title, date, time, duration, etc.)
  2. Paste the Google Sheets link in the "Google Sheets Link" field
  3. Click "Schedule Exam"

Done!

Your exam is now scheduled! The system will automatically fetch questions from your Google Sheet when students start the exam.

Troubleshooting

If the exam doesn't load questions:
• Check that you shared the sheet with the correct email
• Ensure the email has Editor permissions (not Viewer)
• Verify all column headers match exactly: question, option1, option2, option3, option4, answer
• Make sure the answer matches one of the options exactly (case-sensitive)
